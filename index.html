Our Story

A place where every
guest feels at home

RTN CAFE was born from a simple belief — that great food and genuine warmth should be available right in your neighbourhood. Nestled near Vidyanjali School in Coconut Garden, Hebbal, we set out to create a space where locals could unwind, connect, and savour something truly delicious.

From our crispy burgers and hand-rolled momos to our handcrafted coffees and fresh-squeezed juices, every item on our menu is prepared with quality ingredients and honest care. We believe the best meals are the ones shared in good company — and that is exactly what we strive to offer, every single evening.
